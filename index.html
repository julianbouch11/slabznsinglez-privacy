SlabzNSinglez data & privacy

This is a private tool for the seller who owns the configured SlabzNSinglez shipping policies. It prepares CSV files; it does not publish listings or retrieve buyer orders, addresses, messages, or payment information.

On your device

The imported CSV, photos, matches and shipping choices are saved in this browser’s IndexedDB. eBay authorization tokens are encrypted in secure, HttpOnly cookies, renewed on the server, and expire after at most 30 days. There is no separate app account or account database. Clearing site data removes this device’s saved work and connection.

Sync between your devices

Connecting the same eBay seller account on both devices syncs one workspace through private Vercel Blob storage. It contains your CSV, listing matches, uploaded image URLs and expiry dates, prices, and shipping choices, keyed by an opaque account hash. Only an authorized session for that seller can read or update it. Original photo files and pending uploads stay on the device where you selected them; photo URLs sync after uploading to eBay. Seller tokens are never stored in the shared workspace. Changes sync while the page is open and online. Conflicting edits require choosing a copy.

Images and shipping policies

Connected photo uploads go to eBay Picture Services. Unused images have an expiry date returned by eBay. The app blocks export when a known eBay image has less than 48 hours remaining. eBay retains images used in active listings according to its policies. Downloading or removing an image from the workspace never requests its deletion from eBay. The app reads the two configured shipping policies to check their names and services.

Account deletion and disconnecting

Signed eBay account-deletion notifications create a private record containing only a salted hash and a deletion flag. No notification username, user ID, account token, or full payload is retained. This record blocks the deleted account’s sessions across server restarts. The shared server workspace is deleted before acknowledging the notification. When a connected browser next checks in, its authorization cookies and associated saved workspace are cleared. A closed or offline browser cannot be remotely erased; clearing happens when it returns online. The app checks on opening, returning to the page, and once a minute while visible.

Disconnect revokes the app’s eBay authorization and clears its cookies. It keeps your local and shared CSV workspace. You can also revoke access through eBay’s third-party app settings and clear this site’s data in your browser.
